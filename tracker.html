⚡ Main Model: Gemini 3.8 Flash

Used for primary high-speed candidate analysis and live interview copilot actions.

  • Extracts technical skills, experience years, and impact metrics.
  • Calculates candidate-to-JD match score (0–100%).
  • Identifies inconsistencies, exaggerated claims, and employment gaps.
  • Generates live, real-time follow-up questions during interviews.
🛡️ Fallback: Gemini 3.6 Flash

Automatic failover redundancy for maximum reliability during judging.

  • Auto-failover if Gemini 3.8 encounters rate limits or latency.
  • Identical structured JSON output schema for zero client errors.
  • Built-in deterministic offline fallback ensuring the demo never breaks.
